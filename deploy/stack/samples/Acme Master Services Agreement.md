# Master Services Agreement

**Between:** Northwind Trading LLC ("the Client") and Acme Holdings Ltd ("Acme").
**Effective date:** 1 March 2024.

## 1. Term

This agreement runs for an initial term of three years from the effective
date and renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either
party gives notice under section 4.

## 2. Services

Acme provides hosted inventory management, nightly data export and second
line support during Libyan business hours, as described in Schedule A.

## 3. Fees

The annual renewal fee is 4,500 USD, payable each March within thirty days
of invoice. Support beyond the included forty hours a quarter is billed at
85 USD per hour. Fees are exclusive of VAT.

## 4. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience by giving the
other party 90 days written notice. Notice must be sent to the addresses in
Schedule B and takes effect from the date of receipt.

## 5. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Disputes are
referred first to mediation in London and then to arbitration under the
LCIA rules.
