# Amendment No. 1 to the Master Services Agreement

**Parties:** Northwind Trading LLC and Acme Holdings Ltd.
**Effective date:** 1 January 2025.

This amendment modifies the Master Services Agreement dated 1 March 2024.
Except as set out below, the agreement continues unchanged.

## Amendment to section 4, Termination

The notice period for termination for convenience is extended from 90 days
to 120 days written notice. The change applies to any notice served on or
after the effective date of this amendment.

## Amendment to Schedule A, Services

Acme adds a weekly reconciliation report to the nightly data export at no
additional fee. The annual renewal fee of 4,500 USD is unchanged.

## Signatures

Signed for Northwind Trading LLC and for Acme Holdings Ltd on 18 December
2024.
